## Kaynak kuralları

- Tek nesne: kahraman gemisi. Ada, filo, HUD, metin yok.
- **3/4, alçak kamera** — gövde uzun çizgi, yelken üçgeni anıt gibi.
- Stüdyo `#aea49a`. Omurga alt kenara oturur.
- `NOT photoreal`. Müze ahşabı yok. Olgun lotus pembesi yok. Hazine-altını yok.

```text
Using Gemini, generate this as an image — 16:9 landscape, high quality, game-ready concept for image-to-3D.
Pick the best model for stylized-cartoon next-gen game graphics (bright, glossy stylized 3D look — soft-shaded, rounded, colorful, NOT photoreal, NOT photographic).

A SINGLE original Bronze Age HOUSE-GALLEY seen from a dramatic three-quarter bow angle, camera slightly LOW so the ship feels monumental and fills the frame. This is a hero game prop, a home that is also a ship — not a toy boat, not a simple banana hull, not a war penteconter packed with oars in the water.

Long straight sun-bleached wooden hull (#c8b49a) with a fuller midships so the deck reads as a livable room, dark wood shadow in the plank seams (#8a7358). High carved gunwale, a proud upturned sternpost, a simple ram at the bow. Two HUGE RAISED Egyptian WEDJAT (Eye of Horus) amulets carved in high relief from the bow planks — kabarık wood standing proud of the hull, almond lid, tear-curl, falcon cheek mark, lapis pupil (#1f6fa8), charcoal kohl, cream sclera; RUNIC incised glyphs cut into the relief (dark grooves #8a7358). No statue figurehead, no hanging ankh, no pyramid. A tall mast and a LARGE square linen sail already SET (#efe6d2), cloth slack with soft folds, not taut, not furled. Ochre rope (#c9a877) from mast to hull.

Midships on the near side: a ceremonial THRESHOLD — a short gangplank between two posts with a rope lintel, the door from shore onto the home-deck. On the deck a rhythmic row of twelve clay amphorae, the ship's pantry. A tiller at the stern. Oars shipped along the far rail, not in water.

The keel sits on the bottom edge of the frame. Isolated on a perfectly flat even studio backdrop painted #aea49a warm beige. No floor grid, no horizon, no island, no second ship, no crew, no lotus flowers, no HUD, no text, no watermark.

Look: stylized-cartoon next-gen game graphics, soft-shaded rounded forms, clean flat colors, cool blue shadows never black. The wood is cooler and paler than warm gold — the one calm cool anchor. Stylized antique, NOT a museum-accurate reconstruction, NOT photoreal wood grain, NOT treasure gold fittings, NOT ripe lotus pink. Original ship design only — no logos, no brand marks, no existing Odyssey game or film resemblance, no on-screen text.
```
