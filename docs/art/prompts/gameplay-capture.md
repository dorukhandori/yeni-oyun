# Şablon — Gameplay-capture klip (`media`) **[K]**

> **Sınıf:** `media` — makaledeki *"gameplay-style footage"*.
> **Çıktı:** video 16:9, ~6–8 sn, tek kesintisiz çekim · **Hedef:** `art-source/media/`
> Trailer'dan farkı: kurgu yok. Amaç "bu oyun böyle oynanıyor" demek.
> Ortak bloklar: `_anatomy.md`.

## Prompt

```text
Using Higgsfield, generate this as a video — 16:9, ~7 seconds, high quality, single unbroken shot, no cuts. Pick the best model for stylized-cartoon next-gen game graphics (bright, glossy stylized 3D look — soft-shaded, rounded, colorful, NOT photoreal).

Third-person gameplay capture, over-the-shoulder camera trailing the player character at a steady distance, slight handheld game-camera sway. Sun-drenched Aegean shore at golden hour, ankle-deep turquoise water.

Player character (fully original design, no resemblance to any existing game or film character): Doryseus — a lean weathered Bronze Age captain, a simple stylized figure with a small head and readable shoulder line, sun-worn off-white linen tunic with a faded ochre band, leather belt and sandals, short dark beard, a plain cloth satchel at his hip. No armour, no weapon.

Gameplay HUD (clean, cartoon-crisp, thin gold line-work on sun-bleached pale wood): top-left a satchel icon with four slots, two of them filled, top-right a delivery tally, a slim sun-arc indicator, and a compass arrow pointing toward the ship. No health bar, no enemies, no minimap.

Setting: a cluster of lotus blossoms on broad green lily pads in ankle-deep turquoise shallows, reed beds at the edge, golden sand beyond, the sun-bleached beached ship visible in the middle distance.

Motion (7s, one continuous take): Doryseus wades up to a cluster of three lotus plants — one closed pale bud, one half-open showing pink inside, one fully open white-and-pink bloom — pauses, bends and holds for a beat, picks ONLY the fully open one, the tally ticks up, and he turns toward the ship as the shot ends. Foam ripples out from every step.
Camera stays behind the shoulder throughout, subtle squash-and-stretch on the picking motion.

Look: sun-drenched Aegean island at golden hour — turquoise shallows shading into lapis blue, white foam lines, golden sand, white-and-pink lotus that looks faintly lit from within, broad green lily pads, sun-bleached wood. COOL BLUE shadows (never black), turquoise bounce off the water, soft bloom, warm color grade, soft-shaded stylized surfaces with low surface noise. Original characters and world only — no logos, no brand marks, no real game titles, no on-screen text beyond the game HUD.
```

3 üretim, her biri tek eylem: **topla** / **teslim et** / **pus yükselirken yürü**. Sosyal kesitler bunlardan çıkar.
