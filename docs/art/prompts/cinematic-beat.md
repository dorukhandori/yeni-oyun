# Şablon — Sinematik beat (`media`) **[K]**

> **Sınıf:** `media` — makaledeki *"cinematic moments"* / *"cinematic beats"*.
> **Çıktı:** video 16:9, ~5–8 sn · **Hedef:** `art-source/media/`
> Gameplay değil: kamera serbest, HUD yok. Tek bir duyguyu taşır.
> **[?]** Anlatı hedefi `docs/design/` GDD'sinden gelecek — aşağıdaki beat varsayımdır, onay bekliyor.
> Ortak bloklar: `_anatomy.md`.

## Beat'in konusu

Odysseia IX'un dramatik çekirdeği: **lotusu yiyen adam eve dönmek istemez.** Aşağıdaki plan bu anı taşır — unutma tamamlanır, sonra gemi onu geri çağırır.

## Prompt

```text
Using Higgsfield, generate this as a video — 16:9, ~7 seconds, high quality, cinematic. Pick the best model for stylized-cartoon next-gen game graphics (bright, glossy stylized 3D look — soft-shaded, rounded, colorful, NOT photoreal).

Cinematic camera, not gameplay: a slow low push-in past broad green lily pads toward Doryseus sitting down in the ankle-deep turquoise shallows, an open lotus in his hands, his back to the ship. Shallow depth of field, foreground lotus blossoms slipping out of focus.

Character (fully original design, no resemblance to any existing game or film character): Doryseus — a lean weathered Bronze Age captain, a simple stylized figure with a small head and readable shoulder line, sun-worn off-white linen tunic with a faded ochre band, leather belt and sandals, short dark beard, a plain cloth satchel set down beside him. No armour, no weapon. Slack and unhurried — a man who has stopped being in a hurry to leave.

Setting: the lotus shallows of the island at golden hour — clusters of white-and-pink lotus blossoms on lily pads, reed beds, golden sand beyond, and the sun-bleached beached ship far behind him, small in frame.

No HUD, no interface, no text.

Motion (7s): a soft milk-white haze rises and floods the frame, saturation drains away, and the edges of the reeds and lotus melt into bloom until almost nothing is readable — then, in the last second, the cool pale shape of the distant ship resolves faintly through the haze, the only clear thing left.

Look: sun-drenched Aegean island at golden hour dissolving into milk-white haze — turquoise shallows, golden sand, white-and-pink lotus that looks faintly lit from within, green lily pads, sun-bleached wood. COOL BLUE shadows (never black), turquoise bounce off the water, soft bloom, warm color grade, heavy rising haze in the second half. Original characters and world only — no logos, no brand marks, no real game titles, no text.
```

## Varyasyon

2 üretim. Sinematik beat pahalı bir teslimdir; anlatı onayı gelmeden çoğaltma.
